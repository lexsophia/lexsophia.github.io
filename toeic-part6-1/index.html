Facilities Department Announcement

The east entrance of the Hawthorne Office Center will be closed from January 12 through January 16 while new security gates are installed. During this period, all employees and visitors should [79] the west entrance on Pine Street.

Security staff will be stationed there throughout business hours to assist visitors. [80] Employees are advised to allow a few extra minutes when arriving in the morning because temporary lines may form.

Delivery drivers should continue to use the loading area behind the building. [81], large deliveries scheduled for January 13 must be moved to another date because the rear access road will be resurfaced that day.

We apologize for any inconvenience and appreciate your [82] during this temporary closure.

正解・解説を見る

正解：A. use ／ vocabulary

入口を利用するという意味なので use the west entrance が最も自然です。

正解：B. Temporary signs will direct people from the parking area to the correct doors. ／ sentence insertion

西側入口を使う来訪者への案内として、駐車場から正しい入口へ誘導する仮設標識の文が自然につながります。

QUESTION 03

正解：A. As a result ／ transition words

後方道路が舗装工事される結果、大型配送を別日に移す必要があるため As a result が適切です。
