Sunnyvale Apartments — Now Leasing

Sunnyvale Apartments is now leasing one- and two-bedroom units for occupancy starting December 1. Monthly rent starts at $950 for a one-bedroom unit and $1,300 for a two-bedroom unit, including water and parking.

All units feature a modern kitchen, in-unit laundry facilities, and access to a shared fitness center. A community room can be reserved free of charge by residents for private events.

An open house will be held on Saturday, November 14, from 10:00 A.M. to 4:00 P.M. Visitors can tour model units and speak with the leasing staff. Applications submitted during the open house will have the $50 application fee waived.

Sunnyvale Apartments — 入居者募集中

Sunnyvale Apartments では、12月1日から入居可能な1ベッドルームおよび2ベッドルームの物件を募集しています。家賃は1ベッドルームが月950ドルから、2ベッドルームが月1,300ドルからで、水道代と駐車場代が含まれています。
