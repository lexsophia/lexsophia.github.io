Metro Career Expo — September 12

Riverside Convention Hall, 10:00 A.M.–4:00 P.M.
More than 60 employers from technology, finance, retail, and hospitality will attend. Admission is free for students and recent graduates who register online by September 10. Walk-in admission is $8. Resume-review appointments are available in 20-minute time slots and must be booked in advance.

From: Elena Park, Campus Recruiter
To: Marketing Club Members
Subject: Metro Career Expo

Our company will have a booth at the Metro Career Expo next Saturday. I will be there from 10:00 A.M. until 1:30 P.M., and my colleague Jordan Lee will take over afterward. If you plan to stop by, bring a printed résumé; we can give brief feedback between scheduled interviews. Please note that our company is not participating in the official resume-review appointments listed on the event website.

QUESTION 01

Who can enter the expo for free?
正解・解説を見る

正解：B. Students and recent graduates who register by September 10 ／ 詳細

9月10日までにオンライン登録した学生と卒業して間もない人は無料と案内されています。

質問の和訳：誰が無料で入場できますか。

QUESTION 02

正解：A. A printed résumé ／ 複合推論

Ms. Park はクラブ会員に bring a printed résumé と直接伝えています。

質問の和訳：Parkさんはマーケティングクラブの会員に何を持ってくるよう勧めていますか。

QUESTION 03
