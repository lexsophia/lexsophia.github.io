Part 7 練習問題（Quote request: conference brochures）

次の文章を読み、各質問に最も適した答えを選んでください。

From: Lena Brandt, Events Coordinator, Verve Media
To: Solstice Print Group
Subject: Quote request: conference brochures
Date: March 2

Verve Media is preparing brochures for the Coastal Marketing Summit on April 14. We need 1,500 copies of a 12-page, full-color brochure printed on recycled paper and delivered to our Bristol office no later than April 7. Could you send a quote by March 6? We would also like to know whether a discount is available for orders of more than 1,000 copies.

From: Tomas Rivera, Account Manager, Solstice Print Group
To: Lena Brandt
Subject: RE: Quote request: conference brochures
Date: March 4

Thank you for your inquiry. The price includes free delivery to Bristol, and orders of 1,000 copies or more automatically qualify for a 10 percent discount, which has already been applied. Our recycled stock is on back order and is not expected until March 27. Because printing takes five business days, we cannot guarantee delivery before April 7 on that paper. Standard coated stock is available immediately at the same price. Please let us know your choice by March 11.

QUESTION 01

What does Ms. Brandt ask about in her e-mail?
正解・解説を見る

正解：B. Whether reduced pricing applies to large orders ／ paraphrased detail

Ms. Brandt は1,000部を超える注文に割引があるか尋ねています。reduced pricing は discount の言い換えです。

質問の和訳：Brandtさんはメールで何について尋ねていますか。

QUESTION 02

What is suggested about the quoted price?
正解・解説を見る

正解：C. It already reflects the discount Ms. Brandt asked about. ／ 複合推論

返信には10％割引が already been applied とあり、提示価格には依頼した割引がすでに反映されています。

質問の和訳：提示された価格について何が示唆されていますか。

QUESTION 03

What will most likely happen if Ms. Brandt chooses the recycled paper?
正解・解説を見る

正解：A. The brochures may arrive after the requested date. ／ 推論

再生紙の入荷は3月27日予定で印刷に5営業日かかり、4月7日までの配送を保証できないため、希望日より遅れる可能性があります。

質問の和訳：Brandtさんが再生紙を選んだ場合、何が起こる可能性が高いですか。
