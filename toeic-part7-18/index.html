Part 7 練習問題（Invoice 8841）

次の文章を読み、各質問に最も適した答えを選んでください。

From: Mira Shah, Office Manager
To: Accounts Payable
Subject: Invoice 8841

I reviewed the attached invoice from GreenLine Office Supply. Most of the order arrived on Tuesday, but the six adjustable monitor stands listed on the invoice were not included in the delivery. GreenLine told me this morning that the stands are temporarily out of stock and will be shipped separately next week. Please process payment for the items we received, but hold payment for the stands until they arrive.

Invoice 8841 — GreenLine Office Supply

Printer paper: 20 boxes — delivered
Desk organizers: 12 — delivered
Adjustable monitor stands: 6 — back ordered
Delivery fee: waived for orders over $500
Payment terms: due within 30 days of delivery

QUESTION 01

Which item has not yet arrived?
正解・解説を見る

正解：C. Monitor stands ／ 詳細

メールと請求書の両方で adjustable monitor stands が未着・back ordered と示されています。

質問の和訳：まだ届いていない品目はどれですか。

QUESTION 02

Why will payment for part of the invoice be delayed?
正解・解説を見る

正解：B. Some goods are still on back order. ／ 複合推論

Mira は未着のスタンドが届くまで、その分の支払いを保留するよう依頼しています。

質問の和訳：請求書の一部の支払いが保留されるのはなぜですか。

QUESTION 03

What can be inferred about the order total?
正解・解説を見る

正解：A. It is more than $500. ／ cross-passage detail

請求書で500ドルを超える注文は配送料免除とあり、実際に delivery fee が waived なので合計は500ドル超と推測できます。

質問の和訳：注文の合計金額について何が推測できますか。
